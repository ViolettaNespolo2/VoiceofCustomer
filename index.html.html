01 · Important note

Before reading
the data

⚠ Read this first

The data should be interpreted with care and always in the context of real customer outcomes and commercial reality. A meaningful portion of responses likely comes from customers under commercial pressure or who have not yet fully achieved expected ROI — this can naturally amplify frustration.

Some signals around price sensitivity or feature prioritization may be skewed if viewed in isolation. Use this feedback as a guide to identify themes and opportunities, while grounding conclusions in validated customer success.

02 · About this report

What, why,
how

What

The VoC analysis captures direct feedback from customers to understand experiences, expectations, needs, and pain points — identifying recurring themes that influence satisfaction, usability, and overall perception.

Why

Understanding the customer is at the core of our strategy. VoC insights help us make more informed decisions — prioritizing improvements, addressing pain points, and aligning future developments with real customer needs.

How

Anonymous survey collected February 2026. Both aggregated quantitative and qualitative insights reviewed for trends, patterns, and recurring themes.

Overall averages may be slightly skewed due to a small number of extremely biased responses that do not fully reflect broader customer perception. These responses were retained to preserve sample size integrity and ensure all feedback was represented.

23 · Conclusion

Three
conclusions

Software maturity is a gap to close quickly

Eidos shows the largest expectation gap, but how-to requests and feature asks signal genuine engagement. As the platform matures and training expands, satisfaction scores have strong room to recover.

Stronger value selling could shift price perception

The challenge stems from customer fit misalignment and limited understanding of LFAM complexity. The value is there — it isn't translated into a clear customer proposition. ROI docs and TCO narratives can close this gap.

Materials represent an underleveraged growth channel

17/19 customers already purchase materials from Caracol. A proactive materials communication approach reinforces one-stop-shop positioning while reducing third-party sourcing risk.
